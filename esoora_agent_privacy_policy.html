Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 2025

Ethio Clicks ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Esoora Delivery Agent mobile application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application to coordinate deliveries and assist in the platform's order fulfillment process.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect via the App includes:

Personal Data

Personally identifiable information, such as your name, address, email address, telephone number, vehicle registration details, driver's license information, and bank account details for payouts, that you voluntarily give to us when you register with the App.

Geo-Location Information

We require access to and track location-based information from your mobile device, either continuously or while you are using the App in order to assign delivery orders efficiently and provide live tracking to our customers. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device's settings, though this may severely impact your ability to receive or fulfill orders.

Device Access

We may request access or permission to certain features from your mobile device, including your mobile device's camera (e.g., for uploading proof of delivery or personal identification), storage, notifications, and location services. If you wish to change our access or permissions, you may do so in your device's settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

Having accurate information permits us to provide a reliable platform for your delivery operations. Specifically, we may use information collected about you via the App to:

  • Create and manage your agent account.
  • Facilitate order assignments, track delivery progress, and confirm successful deliveries.
  • Process payments, calculate commissions, and remit earnings to your bank account.
  • Email or notify you regarding order status, operational updates, and platform changes.
  • Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve platform efficiency for our delivery partners.
  • Verify your identity, qualifications, and monitor against fraudulent or criminal activity.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations. Your information may be disclosed as follows:

Customers

When you participate in an order lifecycle and are dispatched to deliver an order, we share your name, vehicle details, and real-time location with the customer to facilitate smooth handoffs.

Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third parties that perform services for us or on our behalf, including payment processing, identity verification, background checks, hosting services, and customer support.

4. Security of Your Information

We use administrative, technical, and physical security measures to help protect your personal information. While we have taken reasonable steps to secure the information you provide to us, please be aware that despite our efforts, no security measures are perfect or impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against any interception or other type of misuse.
